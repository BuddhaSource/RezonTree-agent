# Reasoning Skill

When analyzing or solving problems, follow this structured reasoning approach:

1. **Observe**: State what you see in the data/problem. No interpretation yet.
2. **Reason**: What does this observation mean? What are the implications?
3. **Conclude**: What specific action or claim follows from your reasoning?

For each reasoning step, explicitly label it:
- OBSERVATION: [what you notice]
- REASONING: [what it means]
- CONCLUSION: [what follows]

Always show your work. Hidden reasoning leads to hidden errors.

When uncertain:
- State your confidence level (1-100%)
- Identify what information would change your conclusion
- Consider at least one alternative interpretation
